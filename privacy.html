Privacy Policy

Owner Statement Poster is a private, internal tool used only by Doug Donia and the real estate companies he owns or manages.

What it accesses: the QuickBooks Online companies its owner connects, and the property manager statements and documents he provides.

What it stores: the QuickBooks connection tokens and records of the entries it posts, on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on a hosted server.

What it shares: nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to Intuit (QuickBooks Online) to record the owner's own transactions, and to Anthropic (Claude) when a statement or document needs to be read.

Removal: disconnecting the app in QuickBooks revokes its access. Stored data is deleted on request.
